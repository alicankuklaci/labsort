What we store

Lab Sort: Liquid Sorting Game (developer: Alican Kuklaci) does not create a separate account. If you play signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, our leaderboard and duel servers store your player ID, display name, country, your level results (moves and stars), your duel rating and duel match results. Progress, coins and settings stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to alicankuklaci@gmail.com with the subject “Lab Sort data deletion”.
  2. Include the display name shown in the game (Ranks or Duel screen) and whether you play on Android or iOS.
  3. We confirm by email once your data is deleted.

You can also send us a message, but please do not post personal information there, as messages there are public.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted within 30 days of your request: your player ID, display name, country, level results, duel rating and duel match history. You are removed from the leaderboard and duel rankings.

Kept: nothing that identifies you. IP addresses used to limit abuse are deleted from server logs within 30 days in any case. Purchase records are held by Google Play or the App Store, not by us.

To stop sending new data, sign out of Google Play Games or Game Center; the game then plays fully offline.
