Privacy Policy

Lab Sort: Color Puzzle · Effective October 6, 2026 · Türkçe

Summary

Lab Sort does not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, settings (sound, language, accessibility) and purchases are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

Advertising

The free version of the game may show ads served through AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners. To deliver and measure ads, these providers may collect device and usage information such as your device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking when asked), IP address, device model, operating system and ad interactions. Their use of this information is governed by their own policies, for example the AppLovin Privacy Policy. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings, and the Remove Ads purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Send us a message.
