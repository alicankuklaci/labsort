Privacy Policy

Lab Sort: Color Puzzle · Effective October 13, 2026

Summary

Lab Sort does not require an account and never asks for your name or email address. If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the game takes part in the online leaderboard and lets you play online duels, both described below; otherwise your game progress stays on your device. The game currently shows no ads.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, settings (sound, language, accessibility) and purchases are saved locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Only the leaderboard and duel information below is sent to us.

Online leaderboard

If you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, the game sends our leaderboard server your player ID and display name on that service, your country (shown as a flag; worked out from your IP address or your device region) and the results of the levels you finish (the moves you made and the stars earned). We use this only to show and protect the rankings. Your IP address is also used to limit abuse and is deleted from our logs within 30 days. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Country lookup: IP Geolocation by DB-IP.

Duels (online play)

Duels use the same sign-in. When you play a duel, the game connects to our duel server and sends a sign-in token that links the match to your player ID, display name and country, the app version, the duel character you picked and what you do in the match (your moves and undos, giving up, and rematch requests). If you play with a friend, the game also sends the 6-character room code you create or enter; an invite link contains only that code and is shared through the app you choose. Your opponent sees your display name, country flag, character and duel rating. We store your duel rating and match results to run matches, keep them fair and show the duel rankings. Your IP address is handled as for the leaderboard (used to limit abuse, deleted from our logs within 30 days). We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. To have your leaderboard and duel data deleted, send us a message with your display name; signing out of Game Center or Google Play Games stops the game from taking part in either.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

Advertising

Lab Sort currently shows no ads, and no advertising SDK in the game collects data. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before then.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
